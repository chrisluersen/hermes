Hermes

A personal automation assistant.

Hermes is a personal, single-user automation assistant that runs locally on the owner's own computer. It connects to the owner's Google Account so the owner can read and send their own email, manage their own calendar, and work with their own files and documents through a command interface.

What Hermes does with your Google Account

When authorized, Hermes can:

  • Read and send Gmail messages, and read Gmail settings.
  • Read and create Google Calendar events.
  • Read, upload, and organize files in Google Drive.
  • Read and edit Google Docs and Google Sheets.
  • Read Google Contacts.
  • Read and manage Google Tasks.
  • Read YouTube subscription information.

Access is granted only by the account owner, and only for the owner's own data.
